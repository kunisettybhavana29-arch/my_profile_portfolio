About

I started in enterprise IT services and client-facing business research, where my job was to understand what a client needed and pass it on clearly. That is the part of business analysis I enjoy most: asking the right questions, writing the requirement down so no one can misread it, and checking afterwards that what was built is what was asked for.

I write BRDs, SRS documents and user stories with Gherkin acceptance criteria, map process gaps, build wireframes in Figma, and use SQL, Excel and Power BI to look at business data. I have worked in both Agile Scrum and Waterfall, and with stakeholders across six international geographies. My domain exposure covers logistics, healthcare SaaS, InsurTech, FinTech, EdTech and e-commerce.
